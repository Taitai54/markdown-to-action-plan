# Product Direction

## Product Thesis

Markdown to Action Plan should become a knowledge-to-practice system, not only a document-to-checklist converter. Its value is a reliable bridge from source material to usable judgment: preserve what the source means, explain the mental model, show when an idea applies, demonstrate it in a realistic situation, and provide a way to try and verify it.

## Primary User

A solo builder, operator, or knowledge worker who saves books, PDFs, Markdown notes, and YouTube transcripts and wants to turn them into decisions, behaviors, or implementations.

## Job To Be Done

When I capture valuable source material, I want to understand the ideas and know exactly how to apply them in my context, so I can act on the knowledge instead of merely storing or summarizing it.

## Hypothesis

Requiring each important idea to include meaning, use conditions, a concrete example, a practical action, and verification will reduce generic plans and manual rewriting while improving the reader's ability to apply the source without reopening it.

## Delivery Sequence

1. Practical transformation contract: complete.
2. Evaluation fixtures and rubric: complete as a manual rubric.
3. Large-source chunking: complete as client-side heading/paragraph chunk selection.
4. Provenance and source gaps: partially complete; source type and YouTube URL/timestamp preservation exist, but claim-level provenance and PDF page locations remain.
5. Input and retrieval context: next major slice.
6. Practice and feedback loop: refine loop exists; exercises and lightweight application signals remain.
7. Structured knowledge objects: deferred until the output contract proves its value.
